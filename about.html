About BlueGreen

A licensed environmental research and consultancy firm in Gazipur, Bangladesh.

Who we are

Our team combines environmental engineering, environmental science, civil and water resources engineering, and GIS. We help industries meet environmental requirements, and we help students and researchers do better work.

We are based beside the DUET campus in Gazipur and work on site in Bangladesh. Analysis, reporting and training can also be delivered online, in English, for partners elsewhere.

Mission

To provide reliable, practical environmental consultancy and research support that helps industries operate responsibly and helps researchers grow.

Vision

A Bangladesh where industry and nature thrive together, backed by sound science and skilled people.

Values

Integrity, scientific accuracy, and respect for people and the environment.

Why clients choose us

  • Academic roots. We work close to the university research community at DUET, Gazipur.
  • Practical focus. We deliver designs, reports and training that people can use.
  • Support from start to finish. From the first idea to the final report or published paper.
  • Clear communication. Plain language, written scopes and fees, and answers within one working day.
