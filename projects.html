In progress

ETP design and IEE report, recycled paper factory, Sirajganj

ETP design, IEE report

We are designing the effluent treatment plant and preparing the Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) report for a recycled paper factory in Sirajganj.

Work of this kind usually starts with the wastewater the factory really produces, then moves to sizing the treatment units, the baseline survey and the environmental management plan.

What the work covers

  • Characterising the effluent
  • Designing the treatment units
  • Baseline survey and impact assessment
  • Environmental management plan
